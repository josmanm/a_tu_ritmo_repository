# Simon master view specification

Approved reference: `Assets/Art/GenerationRequests/simon_master_hybrid/output/simon_master_hybrid_v3.png`.

## Layout

- Canvas reference: 1280x800, UGUI `CanvasScaler`.
- Top HUD: score, level/progress, three functional heart lives, analog clock with time bar, menu.
- Compass guide: compact horizontal sequence above the board.
- Board: centered, four interactive zones with editable labels D, G, E and C.
- Pulse rings: decorative feedback around the board, generated by Unity graphics rather than a baked screen image.
- Bottom actions: primary play button and secondary replay button.
- No permanent panel between board and bottom actions.

## Implementation split

- Unity UI: all text, labels, buttons, timers, score, lives, compass dots, progress bars, pulse rings, feedback visibility and interaction states.
- Sprite candidates: board frame/zones and button surface artwork only.
- Existing gameplay logic: preserve `SimonGameManager`, `SimonZoneUI`, `TopHudPanelView` and current color labels.
- Do not embed the full screen mockup in the game.

## Accessibility rules

- Keep D/G/E/C labels editable and high contrast.
- Keep zones separated by shape and spacing, not color alone.
- Keep the three hearts only in the functional life bar.
- Use a familiar analog clock icon; do not use an hourglass.
- Keep the background simple and low contrast.
- Hide temporary feedback when inactive.
